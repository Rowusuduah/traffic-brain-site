*The parking shelf — study workflow, worked calculations, and the mistakes that get juniors redlined.*

**The demand triad** every study reconciles: **local code** (legal floor/ceiling) vs **ITE Parking Gen** (observed standalone) vs **ULI Shared Parking** (mixed-use time-sharing) — vs **observed counts** when the site exists. Master the triad and you're immediately useful.

**Spawn as needed:** accumulation/turnover/duration concepts · 85% effective-capacity note `#status/verify` agency threshold · parking mgmt strategies · garage circulation & stall/aisle dimension checks (agency code + FDM/Greenbook where applicable) · ADA parking counts & placement ([[PROWAG and ADA]]) · EV/bike/loading requirements · event/school/church peaking · queuing at access points
**References:** [[ITE Parking Generation]] · [[ULI Shared Parking]] · parking-code notes per jurisdiction
**Report language:** [[MOC - Report Writing]] parking memo pattern
**Common EIT mistakes:** counting only one peak day · mixing gross/leasable floor area bases · applying ULI factors without documenting each · comparing demand to *striped* supply while ignoring reserved/ADA/compact splits · forgetting the code's own shared-parking provision (sometimes better than ULI for approval).
