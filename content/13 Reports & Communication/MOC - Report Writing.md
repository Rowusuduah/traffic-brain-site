**Master pattern for every study report** (TIA/TIS · safety · calming · parking · shared-parking memo · reduction justification): Purpose/authorization → Study area & methodology (mirrors the approved memo) → Existing conditions → Future conditions (background → build) → Analysis → Findings → Recommendations → Appendices (assumptions log, calcs, counts, comment matrix). One report-section note per section as you draft your firsts — steal skeletons from ACCEPTED reports, your firm's best precedent per type.
**Numbers-first discipline:** claims carry values + sources; adjectives get deleted in QC ("significant" → "increases delay 18→42 s (LOS C→E)").
**Assumptions & limitations:** written where the reviewer will find them; a documented assumption is professional, a hidden one is a liability ([[EIT Boundaries Ethics and Liability]]).
**Methodology memo · executive summary · buildout/mitigation sections · comment-response matrices · meeting summaries** — each earns a pattern note after your first accepted one.
**Voice as an EIT — confident, bounded:** state findings plainly within your analysis ("The eastbound left exceeds storage in the PM peak"); route judgment upward explicitly ("Recommend PE review of the signal-modification option"). Never hedge facts; never freelance judgment. In review comments: cite-the-standard phrasing beats opinion phrasing every time ("Per FDM ___, provide ___" vs "should probably").
