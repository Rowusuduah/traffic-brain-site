*The signals shelf, in reading order — timing sheets first, everything else follows.*

**Read first:** [[How to Read a Signal Timing Sheet]] ★ → then [[Clearance Intervals and Ped Timing]].
- [[Signal Warrants (MUTCD Ch 4C)]] — all nine warrants with the data each needs; meeting a warrant ≠ getting a signal.
- [[Intersection Control Evaluation (ICE)]] — FDOT's required control-selection process: stages, roundabout-first culture, free CAP-X/SPICE screening.
- [[Signal Timing Manual (STM2) and Free Training]] — NCHRP 812 free PDF, FDOT Signal 101/102 CBTs, FHWA Signalized Intersections Guide, UDOT open-source ATSPM.
- [[NEMA Phasing and Ring-Barrier]] — the 8-phase numbering (odd = lefts, even = throughs), rings, barriers, and lead-lag; the shared language of every timing sheet.
- [[Offsets and Coordination]] — cycle, split, offset, and reference phase; the wrong-reference-phase trap, time-space thinking, transition basics.
- [[Time-of-Day Plans]] — a signal runs a schedule of plans, not one timing; always match the model period to the plan actually running.
- [[Flashing Yellow Arrow (FYA)]] — the permissive-left display that replaced the doghouse; the left-turn trap and time-of-day protected/permissive modes.
- [[Detection Basics]] — loops vs video vs radar, presence vs passage, recalls, gap-out vs max-out; half of "bad timing" complaints are dead detectors.
- [[Signal Head and Ped Signal Placement]] — MUTCD 4D/4I/4K placement rules, the 20° cone, backplates, APS; prime plan-review territory.
- [[ATSPM Basics]] — high-resolution controller logs turned into coordination, split-failure, and delay plots; retime where the data says.
- [[Cabinets Controllers and Preemption]] — what's in the cabinet (MMU, load switches, detector rack), controller families, preemption vs priority, and the never-open-the-box rule.
**Synchro implementation:** [[Synchro QAQC Checklist]] — timing sheet values must match model settings.
**References:** [[MUTCD]] Part 4 · [[FDOT Traffic Engineering Manual (TEM)]] · maintaining-agency procedures in `03 Agencies` (who owns timing on each road!).
**Common EIT signal mistakes:** modeling ideal timing instead of field timing for existing conditions · ignoring ped mins forcing splits · wrong reference phase → nonsense offsets · treating max green as split.
