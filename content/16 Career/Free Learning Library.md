Each resource actually used → a learning-resource note (takeaway + practice task, or it didn't count). Seed hunt-list by need:

**Florida-official (start here — free AND authoritative):** FDOT CADD office training videos/webinars (ORD/FDOTConnect — the best zero-cost CAD onboarding) · FDOT Design/Traffic Ops training pages · FDOT webinars · CTST/safety office materials.
**Free companions:** [FDOT Traffic Eng & Ops training page](https://www.fdot.gov/traffic/training) — free CBTs (Traffic Signal 101/102, signal timing, adaptive control, performance measures, ITS), recorded TEM symposiums, MUTS course; PDHs available for some · [FDOT Access Management & Site Impact webinar series](https://www.fdot.gov/planning/systems/systems-management/trainings-webinars) — slides + recordings of FDOT staff explaining exactly how they review the TIAs you prepare.
**Federal:** FHWA webinars & courses (NHI has free offerings) · Proven Safety Countermeasure trainings · signal-timing and roundabout resources · PEDSAFE/BIKESAFE.
**Professional bodies:** ITE webinars (member perks via district) · TRB webinars · CUTR (on your campus!) trainings.
**Vendor:** Cubic/Trafficware Synchro tutorials · PTV Vissim academy content · Bentley Learn (ORD/MicroStation paths) · Autodesk (C3D) · Bluebeam University.
**Skills:** Excel/Power Query (ExcelJet-style refs, MyOnlineTrainingHub-type channels) · Python (you're past tutorials — target geopandas/pdfplumber gaps) · QGIS official training.
**YouTube channel-hunting pattern:** search "[software] [task] FDOT" first — Florida-workspace-specific videos exist and beat generic ones.
**Weekly cadence:** ONE resource, one practice task, one note — beats binge-watching a playlist into amnesia.
