**PE (Florida, the main quest):** FE passed → EIT (done) → **PE Civil: Transportation exam** — Florida permits taking it before completing the experience requirement `#status/verify at fbpe.org`; sit early while coursework is fresh → **4 years qualifying experience** under responsible charge (your PE-PTOE experience tracker is the application, pre-written) → FBPE application (references: PEs with direct knowledge — cultivate 3–5 across projects, on purpose). Exam prep: NCEES spec-driven; your daily work IS prep if you note the *why* behind every criterion you apply.
**PTOE (Professional Traffic Operations Engineer, TPCB):** requires PE + ops experience `#status/verify`; the operations-credibility stamp — natural at year ~5–6 if ops/signals is your lane. Prep = signals/HCM/MUTCD depth this vault is already accumulating.
**RSP (Road Safety Professional):** RSP1 (broad, earlier-eligible) → RSP2 (specialist); pairs with the safety/HSM/CMF track — the credential if crash analysis becomes your specialty.
**Sequencing logic:** PE is non-negotiable; PTOE-vs-RSP follows whichever skill domain you actually deepen — decide at your year-3 review.
**Professional orbit:** ITE Florida district + local section (you know FLPRITE from the student side — the leap to professional committees is small and high-yield) · ASCE T&DI optional · TRB papers when practice yields something publishable (your research background makes this unusually reachable — a practice-informed paper is a differentiator few EITs have).
