**Run when:** reviewing or producing temporary traffic control (TTC/MOT) sheets — state or local; confirm the governing standards (MUTCD Part 6 edition, Standard Plans FY) before the first checkbox.

**Governing standard & concept**
- [ ] The cited **102-series index matches the work type** (shoulder work vs lane closure vs flagging vs mobile op vs road closure/detour) — a mismatched index is the #1 catch `#status/verify index numbers against current-FY Standard Plans`
- [ ] Every work phase has a TTC condition; phasing sequence buildable (can crews actually get from Phase 1 to Phase 2?)
- [ ] Typical-section notes vs plan-view layout consistent (speeds, dimensions, device types)

**Tapers, buffers, geometry**
- [ ] Merging/shifting/shoulder taper lengths per the governing formula/table for the **posted (or work-zone) speed** — recompute, don't trust the label `#status/verify formula & tables in MUTCD Part 6 / Std Plans`
- [ ] **Buffer space** (longitudinal, and lateral where used) shown and not repurposed for work area or storage
- [ ] Lane widths through the zone adequate for the traffic mix (trucks); shifts drivable at speed

**Pedestrians & ADA**
- [ ] Continuous, detectable, accessible ped route THROUGH or safely AROUND the zone — closing a sidewalk requires an equivalent route, not a "sidewalk closed" sign at the fence
- [ ] Temporary ramps/surfaces, channelizing devices detectable to cane users; protected from drop-offs and traffic ([[PROWAG and ADA]])
- [ ] Crossings relocated? Then temp signing/markings AND signal ped features (pushbuttons, heads) still work at the new location

**Lane-closure windows & analysis**
- [ ] Allowed closure hours stated on the sheets and backed by an analysis or agency lane-closure policy (demand vs work-zone capacity) — not just copied from the last job `#status/verify agency lane-closure policy/analysis tool`
- [ ] Queue estimate vs upstream reality: does the predicted queue reach an interchange, signal, or curve?

**Devices & readability**
- [ ] Device spacing (cones/drums, sign spacing series) per standard for speed; advance warning distances recomputed
- [ ] Sign legends legible at the plotted scale — if you can't read it on the sheet, the fabricator guesses
- [ ] Arrow boards / PCMS locations logical; existing conflicting signs covered or removed by note

**Signals & existing operations**
- [ ] TTC does not blind or contradict an existing signal (heads visible, detection maintained or spec'd to temporary mode, ped phases handled)
- [ ] Detour routes: receiving streets can take it; turn restrictions signed at both ends; emergency/transit access maintained

**Approval path:** who signs off varies by owner — on FDOT work expect district construction and/or district traffic operations involvement; locals have their own MOT review desks. Name the approver in your memo. `#status/verify per district/agency`

> [!tip]
> FHWA's Work Zone Management Program hub (ops.fhwa.dot.gov/wz) is the free companion library — MUTCD Part 6 guidance, worker safety, work-zone data and webinars. Pair it with [[FDOT Standard Plans]] 102-series and the Design Standards eBooklet for index-by-index intent.

> [!warning]
> A TTC sheet that is "per Index" but doesn't fit the site (driveways inside the taper, no room for the buffer, ped route ignored) is still wrong. Indexes are typicals; the plan must resolve the site.

**Practice question:** night work, one lane of a 45 mph 4-lane closed, sidewalk on the closed side — list every checklist section that fires and the two most likely review comments.
