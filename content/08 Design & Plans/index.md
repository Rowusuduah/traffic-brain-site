**Plan sheet literacy list** (make a one-page "how to read" note per type as you meet them): S&PM · signalization · lighting · ITS · MOT/TTC · typical sections · roadway plans · intersection layouts · driveway connections · ped/bike · school zones · curb ramp details · parking layout/garage circulation/loading/ADA plans.
**Discipline coordination:** what traffic OWES others (design vehicle turns, signal/ITS needs, MOT phasing input, S&PM) and NEEDS from them (drainage inlets vs curb ramps/bulb-outs, utility conflicts w/ poles & loops, structures clearances, lighting circuits, roadway geometry files). One coordination-note per project saves three meetings.
