title: "★ Signing & Pavement Marking (S&PM) Plan Review Checklist"

**Run when:** reviewing/producing S&PM sheets (state or local — confirm governing standards first!).

**Signs**
- [ ] Every regulatory/warning sign justified (MUTCD section noted for anything unusual)
- [ ] Advance placement distances per MUTCD tables for speed
- [ ] Post/panel per Standard Plans 700-series; breakaway & clear-zone sanity ([[AASHTO Roadside Design Guide]])
- [ ] No conflicts: sign-vs-sign spacing, signal head sightlines, vegetation/lighting
- [ ] School zone signing/times per TEM · Street name signs per agency std
**Markings**
- [ ] Lane lines/edge lines: type/width/material per 711 & spec (paint vs thermo)
- [ ] Stop bars & crosswalks: placement/dimensions per Standard Plans; ADA-consistent crossings
- [ ] Arrows: sequence & counts by storage length per standard; RPMs pattern
- [ ] Tapers: lengths per governing formula/standard for speed; transitions signed AND marked consistently
- [ ] Gore/channelization, yield lines (RRFB/roundabout contexts), bike markings per current standards
**Sheet hygiene**
- [ ] Legend matches usage; every callout has an index #; stations/offsets spot-checked; matchline continuity; quantities on sheet foot to tab
**Example application:** first pass = ride the corridor in Google Earth/Street View against the sheets; desk-catches half the misses.
**Practice question:** left-turn bay 275 ft — how many arrows and where (which index answers it)?
**QA/QC reminder:** governing edition (Std Plans FY / MUTCD ed.) written on your review memo header.
