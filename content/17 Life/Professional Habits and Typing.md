# Professional Habits, Time & Typing
**Time:** calendar-block deep work (mornings for analysis; email at set times) · the daily note's "tomorrow's first task" line kills morning flail · one weekly planning pass = [[Weekly Review Routine]] step 6 · learn to give honest estimates by tracking actuals vs guesses (the [[T - Workflow]] "time it actually takes" field).
**Working while learning (you're literally doing a PhD alongside — protect the boundary):** fixed study windows > stolen hours; the vault means job-learning compounds without extra time.
**Typing to 70+ WPM:** 10 min/day on monkeytype/keybr for 6–8 weeks; learn symbols/numbers row properly (engineers type units & code) · master the OS/Office shortcuts that dwarf raw WPM: window snapping, paste-special, F4 repeat, Excel navigation (Ctrl+arrows/shift), Outlook quick steps · text expander for boilerplate (email skeletons from [[Wording Bank and Email Patterns]]).
**Confidence, mechanically produced:** competence → evidence → confidence. The vault is the evidence engine; review [[Lessons Learned System]] wins monthly. Speak at meetings early via prepared micro-contributions (one data point you own). Imposter feelings at month 3 are the norm, not a signal.
**Work-life:** utilization pressure is real — sustainable pace + honest flags beat hero-crash cycles; PTO is compensation, use it.
