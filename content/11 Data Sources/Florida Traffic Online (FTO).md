**What:** FDOT's interactive statewide map of traffic count stations — AADT by year, K/D/T factors, hourly/classification/speed counts, downloadable historical data per station.
**Why:** the free official source for the near-daily lookups: existing volumes for TIAs, design-hour factors for turn-lane and LOS work, multi-year AADT for growth rates.
**When:** existing-conditions volumes, background growth trends, K/D/T retrieval, checking whether a segment has a continuous (telemetered) vs portable station.

No login. Coverage is strongest on the State Highway System; local roads mostly aren't here — go to the county/MPO programs for those. The app URL is an FDOT app-server address and has moved before `#status/verify` — if it 404s, re-enter via the FDOT Traffic Data page.

> [!tip]
> Full pull procedure lives in [[How to Pull AADT and K-D-T Factors from Florida Traffic Online]]. For bulk/batch data (shapefiles, the whole historical database) skip the map and use [[FDOT Traffic Data (TDA)]].

> [!warning]
> The station is not your segment. Check where the station actually sits (which side of the major driveway/intersection) before adopting its AADT for your study link.
