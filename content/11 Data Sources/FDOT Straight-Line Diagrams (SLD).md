**What:** straight-line diagrams — linear, milepost-referenced drawings of features along State Highway System roadways. The online GIS app (slogis.fdot.gov) searches by district, county, roadway ID, or a map click; the SLD Handbook (April 2023 edition `#status/verify`) is the legend for the symbols and abbreviations.
**Why:** the fastest way to get features tied to mileposts on a state road — which is how FDOT references crash locations, project limits, and RCI data.
**When:** converting crash mileposts to real locations (and back), corridor feature inventory, confirming begin/end limits, cross-checking RCI.

No login; diagrams download as PDFs. Gotcha: diagram vintages vary by roadway — check the date block and verify anything decision-critical against current aerials or the field.

> [!tip]
> Keep the roadway ID (8-digit) from the SLD — it's the key that joins FTO stations, RCI extracts, and crash records to the same corridor.
