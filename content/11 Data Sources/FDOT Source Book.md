**What:** FDOT's annual interactive report of Florida transportation performance — VMT, congestion, safety trends, transit/bike/ped metrics, federal performance measures — with segment-level visualization and MPO breakdowns.
**Why:** ready-made, citable trend statistics from an official source; the fastest defensible answer to "is traffic/crashes/congestion up here?"
**When:** report introductions, existing-conditions and context chapters, framing a corridor against the district or MPO trend.

No login. Cite the edition year and the specific measure — the numbers update annually, so a screenshot without a date is a future correction.

> [!tip]
> For the raw data behind a Source Book chart, chase it to the source: counts on [[FDOT Traffic Data (TDA)]], travel time in [[NPMRDS Analytics (RITIS)]].
