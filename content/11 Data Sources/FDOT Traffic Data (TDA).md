**What:** the landing page for all FDOT traffic statistics — historical FTI database download (Access .mdb), AADT and truck-AADT GIS shapefiles, county DVMT reports, CSV daily-volume archives, the Traffic Monitoring Handbook and Project Traffic Forecasting Handbook, and the Non-Motorized Traffic Monitoring Program (bike/ped counts).
**Why:** the bulk-download and documentation counterpart to Florida Traffic Online — and the only statewide source of bicycle/pedestrian count data.
**When:** batch AADT for GIS maps, citing count/forecasting methodology, bike/ped volumes for multimodal sections, pulling a whole county's history at once.

Annual statistics are collected January–December and posted by roughly April the following year `#status/verify cadence`. The handbooks on this page are the methodology citations behind any count or forecast you defend.

> [!tip]
> Need one station, one number? Use [[Florida Traffic Online (FTO)]] instead. Need a mapped layer? The AADT shapefiles here also live as feature services on [[FDOT Open Data Hub]].
