**Role/jurisdiction:** Unincorporated county roads only — distinct from the City of Sarasota, Venice, North Port, and Longboat Key (town straddles the Sarasota/Manatee line). Strong complete-streets/multimodal orientation (2021 Bicycle & Pedestrian Master Plan + Complete Streets Implementation Strategies). Development review runs through **Planning and Development Services** (electronic submittal via Accela); traffic operations through **Traffic Engineering & Operations** under Transportation/Public Works. FDOT **District 1**.

**Known starting points** (resolved 2026-07-08 unless tagged):

## Collect (link each as found; date it)
- **TIA/TIS guidelines:** the [Transportation Planning page](https://www.scgov.net/government/transportation/transportation-planning) links a **Transportation Impact Analysis Methodology** ([PDF, scgov doc 46018](https://www.scgov.net/home/showpublisheddocument/46018/637868219696200000) — 12-pp. scanned document) and **Concurrency Management Systems Regulations** ([PDF, doc 46019](https://www.scgov.net/home/showpublisheddocument/46019/637868219181030000)), plus annual roadway **Level of Service Analysis** reports (2014–2023 editions). Code hook: UDC **§ 124-253 — Transportation Regulatory Provisions** (Article 13, Subdivision or Site Development Plan Regulations). Scope/methodology expectations beyond the posted documents: request from Transportation Planning. (checked 2026-07-08; scgov links verified via Wayback 2025-12-28 snapshot — 403 to bots)
- **Parking study guidelines / parking code (with section #):** UDC (Code of Ordinances **Chapter 124**) **§ 124-120 — Off-Street Parking and Loading** (Article 8, Supplemental Development Regulations) ([Municode Ch. 124 TOC](https://library.municode.com/fl/sarasota_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIICOOR_CH124UNDECO)). No standalone county parking *study* guideline published online — request from Planning and Development Services. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Traffic calming program/guidelines:** **RNTMP Part II (Traffic Calming)** — administered by Traffic Engineering & Operations per **Code § 98-14 and Ordinance 2023-071**; program details and petition form live on the [Traffic Engineering & Operations page](https://www.scgov.net/government/transportation/traffic-engineering-operations). Key mechanics: petitioner must own a parcel abutting/accessing the street; petition needs **≥ 10 signatures** of owners of abutting residential parcels; county then runs a **traffic criteria study** against § 98-14; stakeholder workshop (waivable for feedback signs); survey requiring **two-thirds majority** support of abutting/accessing parcel owners; approved devices = driver speed feedback signs, raised crosswalks, speed cushions, modular mini roundabouts; **petitioners pay the device cost**, county installs/maintains/removes. (checked 2026-07-08; page verified via Wayback 2026-04-17 snapshot — 403 to bots)
- **Complete Streets policy · Vision Zero / Safety Action Plan:** BCC adopted the **2021 Bicycle and Pedestrian Master Plan and Complete Streets Implementation Strategies Plan** in 2021 (with direction to fold them into the UDC) — see [Bicycle and Pedestrian Planning](https://www.scgov.net/government/transportation/transportation-planning/bicycle-and-pedestrian-planning). Safety: **Sarasota County SS4A Safety Action Plan** (with the cities of Sarasota, Venice, North Port and the Town of Longboat Key) — [SS4A Action Plan page](https://www.scgov.net/government/transportation/transportation-planning/safe-streets-for-all-ss4a-action-plan) links the plan document and fact sheet. Regional: Sarasota/Manatee MPO **Destination Zero** [Safety Action Plan](https://www.mympo.org/destination-zero/safety-action-plan) (first adopted 2022-02-28). (checked 2026-07-08; scgov pages verified via Wayback — 403 to bots)
- **Roadway design standards / standard details:** transportation design/detail requirements live in the UDC — **Article 13 (§§ 124-250–124-256**, esp. § 124-253**)** and **Article 18, Appendix F — Transportation Technical Details** ([Municode](https://library.municode.com/fl/sarasota_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIICOOR_CH124UNDECO)); county road construction defaults to the Florida Greenbook and FDOT Standard Specifications per **Chapter 98 — Roads and Bridges** ([Municode](https://library.municode.com/fl/sarasota_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIICOOR_CH98ROBR)). The legacy pre-UDC **Road Construction Technical Manual** (old Ch. 74 technical manuals) still circulates — confirm current applicability with Land Development before relying on it `#status/verify`. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Access management standards:** UDC **§ 124-121 — Access and Circulation** (Article 8) plus the transportation provisions of § 124-253; any work in county right-of-way **including driveway connections** requires a Right-of-Way Use Permit (see permits entry). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Traffic count requirements (seasonal factors accepted?):** yes on seasonal adjustment for published data — the county states raw counts on county roadways are converted to AADT **"using seasonal factors."** Data: **Traffic Count Database System (TCDS)** at [trafficops.scgov.net](https://trafficops.scgov.net/Home/TrafficCount) (interactive, public) and annual count downloads on the [Traffic Operations Data page](https://www.scgov.net/government/public-works/traffic-engineering-operations/traffic-count); **Quarterly Arterial Performance Reports** (BlueTOAD travel-time, segment LOS A–F, segment crashes) in the [Traffic Reports Library](https://www.scgov.net/government/transportation/traffic-engineering-operations/traffic-reports-library). Whether study counts may rely on county factors vs. FDOT factors: confirm at methodology `#status/verify`. (checked 2026-07-08; scgov pages 403 to bots, verified via search + Wayback)
- **Crash/safety dashboard:** the county itself publishes **Annual Crash Reports (2016–2024)** — Traffic Engineering analyzes 10,000–20,000 FLHSMV crash reports per year; the prior-year report posts after June 30 (links on the [TEO page](https://www.scgov.net/government/transportation/traffic-engineering-operations) and Traffic Reports Library). Regional KSI mapping: MPO [Destination Zero safety data](https://www.mympo.org/destination-zero/safety-data). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **GIS / open data portal:** [Sarasota County Enterprise GIS open data (ArcGIS Hub)](https://data-sarco.opendata.arcgis.com/) — streets (with maintaining entity attribute), address points, boundaries, planning layers; county [Maps/GIS page](https://www.scgov.net/residents/maps). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Signal timing procedures & maintaining agency:** county Traffic Operations manages **~273 signalized intersections** through its Traffic Management Center (real-time retiming requests via the Contact Center); the county is party to the 2005 regional ATMS interlocal (FDOT, Sarasota County, Manatee County, City of Sarasota, City of Bradenton) with the shared **Regional TMC located in Manatee County** ([FDOT D1 ITS architecture](https://teo.fdot.gov/architecture/architectures/d1/html/elements/el111.html)). City of Venice maintains its own downtown signs/signals; per-corridor maintaining agency inside Venice and North Port `#status/verify`. No written signal-timing procedures published — request from Traffic Engineering & Operations. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Development review process & submittal requirements:** [Planning and Development Services](https://www.scgov.net/government/planning-and-development-services) — pre-application and development applications submitted electronically via [Online Permitting](https://www.scgov.net/government/planning-and-development-services/online-permitting) (Accela Civic Access + Digital Plan Room); permit records searchable at [building.scgov.net](https://building.scgov.net/); land development overview: [Land Development page](https://www.scgov.net/government/planning-and-development-services/planning-and-zoning/land-development). (checked 2026-07-08; scgov pages 403 to bots, verified via search + Wayback)
- **Permit forms (ROW, driveway):** **Right-of-Way Use Permit** required for any work in county ROW (driveway connections, utilities, sidewalks, drainage — per Ch. 98); applications go through the Accela [Online Permitting](https://www.scgov.net/government/planning-and-development-services/online-permitting) portal (Land Development section handles ROW). No standalone paper form posted — apply online. (checked 2026-07-08)
