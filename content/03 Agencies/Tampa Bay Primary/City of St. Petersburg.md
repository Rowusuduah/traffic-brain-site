**Role/jurisdiction:** Second major city in the region; city streets only — Pinellas County and FDOT District 7 roads run through the city, so always confirm the maintaining agency per corridor before citing standards `#status/verify`. Strong complete streets identity (policy 2015, implementation plan 2019). Development review runs through Planning & Development Services and the Development Review Commission (DRC); right-of-way and infrastructure permits through Engineering & Capital Improvements (ECID); day-to-day signs/signals/markings through Public Works' Stormwater, Pavement & Traffic Operations.

**Known starting points** (resolved 2026-07-08 unless tagged):
- Complete Streets implementation plan located — **May 2019** plan (updates the 2015 plan; council-approved), linked below with policy and appendices.
- Traffic calming program confirmed — criteria and process live on the city's Neighborhood Improvement page (no standalone handbook PDF), linked below.
- The city has **no transportation concurrency**; development pays Multimodal Impact Fees under the Pinellas countywide ordinance, and a traffic study is only triggered on four named congested segments (see TIA entry).
- Vision Zero: the city has no standalone city-adopted Vision Zero plan — the operative safety plan is **Safe Streets Pinellas** (Forward Pinellas, countywide), which St. Petersburg co-developed.

## Collect (link each as found; date it)
- **TIA/TIS guidelines:** no standalone published TIA/TIS manual. The published trigger lives on [Permit & Impact Fees](https://www.stpete.org/business/building_permitting/fees.php): the city has **no transportation concurrency requirements**; a **transportation management plan and possibly a traffic study** may be required only if the project generates **more than 50 new PM peak-hour trips** *and* sits on a listed congested segment — Gandy Blvd (San Martin Blvd–4th St), 38th Ave N (34th St–49th St), 22nd Ave N (I-275–34th St), 54th Ave S (31st St–34th St). Development also pays **Multimodal Impact Fees** under the [Pinellas County Countywide MIF Ordinance](https://www.municode.com/library/fl/pinellas_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIIILADECO_CH150IMFE_ARTIIMUIMFE) (Schedule A suburban; Schedule B downtown and adjacent; redevelopment credits back to the highest use since 1986). Study scoping/methodology expectations are not published — request from Transportation & Parking Management. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Parking study guidelines / parking code (with section #):** LDR (City Code Chapter 16) **§ 16.40.090 — Parking and Loading, Design Standards** ([Municode](https://library.municode.com/fl/st._petersburg/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIISTPECO_CH16LADERE_S16.40.090PALODEST)); parking enforcement is § 16.40.100. No standalone parking *study* guideline is published online — reduction/shared-parking study expectations: request from Planning & Development Services. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Traffic calming program/guidelines:** Neighborhood Traffic Calming program — criteria, process, and per-neighborhood traffic plans on [Improve Your Neighborhood](https://www.stpete.org/residents/community/improve_your_neighborhood.php) (traffic calming section). Key mechanics: local or neighborhood-collector streets posted **below 35 mph**; speeding trigger = **85th percentile more than 8 mph over the posted limit, or over 35 mph** (either direction alone qualifies); cut-through trigger = **more than 20% of daily volume in a single hour** *and* daily volume over 400 vehicles. Toolbox includes speed humps, speed tables, raised intersections, median islands, and chicanes. Requester must own/rent/reside on the subject street; city evaluates on request. No standalone handbook PDF is published — the web page is the program document. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Complete Streets policy · Vision Zero / Safety Action Plan:** [Complete Streets program page](https://www.stpete.org/residents/parking___transportation/complete_streets.php) → [Complete Streets Policy, adopted 2015-11-02 (Admin Policy #020400) (PDF)](https://www.stpete.org/Residents/parking_transportation/Complete%20Streets/Complete%20Streets%20Policy%2011-2-15_final_approved.pdf) · [Complete Streets Implementation Plan, May 2019 (PDF)](https://www.stpete.org/Government/city_initiatives/StPete_CompleteStreetsImplementationPlan_May2019.pdf) (+ [Appendices](https://www.stpete.org/Government/city_initiatives/StPete_CS-ImplementationPlan_Appendices_May%202019.pdf)) · [Neighborhood Greenways Toolbox, Feb 2024 (PDF)](https://www.stpete.org/240214_St%20Petersburg%20Neighborhood%20Greenways%20Toolbox_final.pdf). Vision Zero: no standalone city plan found online — the operative plan is [Safe Streets Pinellas](https://forwardpinellas.org/safestreets/the-action-plan/) ([Action Plan PDF, adopted 2021](https://forwardpinellas.org/safestreets/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/action_plan_V7_2-3-23_508_Compliant-2.pdf), zero-by-2045 goal; SS4A-funded update underway), developed with the county and cities including St. Petersburg. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Roadway design standards / standard details:** no standalone city engineering design manual or standard-details book found published online `#status/verify` — request from Engineering & Capital Improvements. What is public: ECID's reference documents (Temporary Traffic Control, ROW Permit Guidance, utility submittal requirements) on the [ECID directory page](https://www.stpete.org/connect_with_us/directories/engineering_capital_improvements.php), plus LDR Chapter 16 design standards. FDOT Standard Plans govern state roads. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Access management standards:** no standalone access management manual. Driveway/apron work in the right-of-way requires an ECID ROW permit (see permit entry); driveway, apron, and visibility-triangle standards are in LDR Chapter 16 ([Ch. 16 mini-TOC on Municode](https://library.municode.com/fl/st._petersburg/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIISTPECO_CH16LADERE)). FDOT (state roads) and Pinellas County (county roads) control access on their own facilities. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Traffic count requirements (seasonal factors accepted?):** the city does not publish its own count database. Use [Forward Pinellas Traffic Count Maps](https://forwardpinellas.org/document-portal/traffic-count-maps/) — 120+ locations, counted Tue–Thu and **seasonally adjusted** — and FDOT Florida Traffic Online for state roads. Whether the city requires new counts vs. accepting adjusted program counts in a study is not published — confirm at scoping `#status/verify`. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Crash/safety dashboard:** none published by the city itself. Use the Safe Streets Pinellas [High Injury Network dashboard](https://devapps.fehrandpeers.com/devportal/apps/experiencebuilder/experience/?id=89c8c090063b4b20be7e36c383469f73) (HIN = ~4% of the network carrying ~60% of fatal/serious-injury crashes; linked from the [action plan page](https://forwardpinellas.org/safestreets/the-action-plan/)). Signal Four Analytics requires an agency login. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **GIS / open data portal:** [St. Petersburg GeoHub (ArcGIS open data)](https://geohub-csp.opendata.arcgis.com/) (city's official portal per [GIS Maps](https://www.stpete.org/government/transparency/gis_maps.php)) and the [Transportation Department Public Viewer](https://egis.stpete.org/portal/apps/webappviewer/index.html?id=2422543923b74992b4fe1dd5ef619d6e) on egis.stpete.org — network details, speed limits, and proposed bike/ped infrastructure. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Signal timing procedures & maintaining agency:** the **city maintains its own signals** — unlike smaller Pinellas cities, St. Pete does not contract signal maintenance to the county. Traffic Operations (Public Works [Stormwater, Pavement & Traffic Operations](https://www.stpete.org/connect_with_us/directories/stormwater_pavement_traffic_operations.php)) handles signal, sign, and marking maintenance, and the city runs its own [Traffic Control Center (FDOT D7 ITS architecture element)](https://teo.fdot.gov/architecture/architectures/d7/html/elements/el16.html). No written signal-timing procedures document is published — request from the city. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Development review process & submittal requirements:** [Development Review Commission](https://www.stpete.org/government/boards___committees/development_review_commission.php) — hears site plans, special exceptions, and subdivisions under the LDRs; meets the first Wednesday of each month; a pre-application meeting is required before submittal. Applications and checklists: [Planning & Zoning Forms & Applications](https://www.stpete.org/business/planning___zoning/forms___applications.php); building-side review via [Building Permits](https://www.stpete.org/business/building_permitting/building_permits.php) (Construction Services & Permitting). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Permit forms (ROW, driveway):** ECID Right-of-Way Use Permit covers driveway/apron and other ROW work — [ROW Permit Application (ECID_FORM_PERM_007) (PDF)](https://www.stpete.org/ECID_FORM_PERM_007%20-%20ROW%20Permit%20Application%20(1).pdf) · [ROW Permit Guidance (ECID_REF_PERM_031) (PDF)](https://www.stpete.org/ECID_REF_PERM_031%20-%20ROW%20Permit%20Guidance.pdf) · Temporary Traffic Control application and the full form set on the [ECID directory page](https://www.stpete.org/connect_with_us/directories/engineering_capital_improvements.php). (checked 2026-07-08)
