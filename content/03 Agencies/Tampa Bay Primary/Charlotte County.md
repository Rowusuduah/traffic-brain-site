**Role/jurisdiction:** Unincorporated Charlotte County (Port Charlotte, Murdock, Englewood area); Punta Gorda is the county's only municipality and runs its own land development review, but **the county maintains Punta Gorda's traffic signals under contract** (see signals entry). Land development review runs through Community Development (Planning & Zoning); roadway engineering and operations through Public Works. Heavy hurricane-rebuild (Ian) driven workload. State roads (US 41, SR 776, I-75) are FDOT District 1.

**Known starting points** (resolved 2026-07-08 unless tagged):
- Development code is the **Code of Laws and Ordinances, Part III — Land Development and Growth Management** ([Municode](https://library.municode.com/fl/charlotte_county)) — there is no separately-published "TIS manual"; traffic study requirements live in the code (Ch. 3-7 Subdivisions) and site plan submittal materials.
- The MPO here is the **Charlotte County–Punta Gorda MPO** ([ccpgmpo.gov](https://ccpgmpo.gov/)) — its SS4A Comprehensive Safety Action Plan and Vision Zero crash dashboard are the safety data products for the county.
- Confirm FDOT vs. County maintenance per corridor before citing standards (FDOT District 1) `#status/verify` per corridor.

## Collect (link each as found; date it)
- **TIA/TIS guidelines:** no standalone county TIA/TIS guidelines manual published online. Code basis: **§ 3-7-88 — Traffic impact statement** ([Municode](https://library.municode.com/fl/charlotte_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIIILADEGRMA_CH3-7SURE_ARTIVSTIMBECO_S3-7-88TRIMST)) — subdivisions must submit a traffic study of impacts on the county road network incl. site-related intersection improvements at main access points; a justification narrative may substitute for small projects, with the county engineer/planning director deciding if more analysis is required. The [Site Plan Review Brochure (rev. 02/2025, PDF)](https://www.charlottecountyfl.gov/core/fileparse.php/363/urlt/Site-Plan-Review-Brochure.pdf) lists traffic impact statements among site plan application materials. Methodology/scoping expectations: request from the county. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Parking study guidelines / parking code (with section #):** **§ 3-9-79 — Off-street parking and loading facilities**, Ch. 3-9 Zoning, Art. III Special Regulations ([Municode](https://library.municode.com/fl/charlotte_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIIILADEGRMA_CH3-9ZO_ARTIIISPRE_S3-9-79OREPALOFA)). No county parking *study* guideline published online — request from Planning & Zoning. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Traffic calming program/guidelines:** no published traffic calming program, criteria, or petition process found online — the [Maintenance & Operations](https://www.charlottecountyfl.gov/departments/public-works/maintenance-operations/) page mentions traffic calming only as part of sign/marking maintenance work. Request the current process from Public Works `#status/verify`. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Complete Streets policy · Vision Zero / Safety Action Plan:** no county-adopted Complete Streets policy found online `#status/verify`. Safety plan is the MPO's: [CC–PG MPO Comprehensive Safety Action Plan (SS4A)](https://ccpgmpo.gov/mpo-documents/ss4a-action-plan/) — $262k SS4A planning grant (2023), Vision Zero / Safe System framework, exec summary + full plan PDFs on that page (target adoption Dec 2024 per the page). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Roadway design standards / standard details:** [Engineering Design and Construction Standards within County ROW (PDF, 22 pp.)](https://www.charlottecountyfl.gov/core/fileparse.php/363/urlt/engineering-design-and-construction-standards.pdf) — governs all work in county ROW; defaults to FDOT Standard Specifications/Indexes + MUTCD; County Engineer may grant variances. Subdivision pavement/ROW specs: § 3-7-84 (Ch. 3-7, [Municode](https://library.municode.com/fl/charlotte_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIIILADEGRMA_CH3-7SURE_ARTIVSTIMBECO)). Also: [Supplemental Specifications for Traffic Signal and Street Light Installations, March 2025 (PDF)](https://www.charlottecountyfl.gov/file/491/supplemental-specs-traffic-signal-installations-March-2025.pdf) and the Public Works [MOT policy (PDF)](https://www.charlottecountyfl.gov/core/fileparse.php/491/urlt/mot-policy.pdf). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Access management standards:** **Ch. 3-6 Roads and Bridges, Art. IV — Highway Access Control** (§§ 3-6-82 to 3-6-89; highway access standards at § 3-6-85, controlled-access arterials/collectors at § 3-6-86) ([Municode](https://library.municode.com/fl/charlotte_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIIILADEGRMA_CH3-6ROBR_ARTIVHIACCO)). Driveway connections are permitted through the ROW permit (below). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Traffic count requirements (seasonal factors accepted?):** no published count-collection or seasonal-adjustment requirements — request from Public Works. Published volume/LOS data: [2025 Roadway Level of Service (concurrency) report (PDF)](https://www.charlottecountyfl.gov/file/972/roadway-concurrency-report.pdf) — peak-hour two-way LOS by segment, maintained by Transportation Engineering (Public Works). No public count *database* found (the old Transportation Engineering counts page now 403s); FDOT Traffic Online covers state roads. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Crash/safety dashboard:** [CC–PG MPO Vision Zero Crash Data Dashboard](https://ccgis.charlottecountyfl.gov/portal/apps/dashboards/bf553c20578744e39eecd94ac3107f91) (hosted on the county's ArcGIS portal; linked from the MPO SS4A page). No separate county-published crash dashboard found; Signal Four Analytics requires agency login. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **GIS / open data portal:** [Charlotte County GIS](https://www.charlottecountyfl.gov/gis/) — [Interactive Map App Gallery](https://www.charlottecountyfl.gov/departments/community-development/maps-addressing/interactive-maps.stml), [Shapefile Gallery](https://www.charlottecountyfl.gov/gis/shapefile-gallery.stml) (downloads), plus raw file server at [data.charlottecountyfl.gov](https://data.charlottecountyfl.gov/) and ArcGIS REST services (agis3.charlottecountyfl.gov). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Signal timing procedures & maintaining agency:** Public Works **Maintenance & Operations** runs 100+ signalized intersections, 52 incident cameras, detection systems, and 75 mi of fiber ([M&O page](https://www.charlottecountyfl.gov/departments/public-works/maintenance-operations/)); central control is the **Charlotte County/Punta Gorda ATMS** ([FDOT D1 ITS architecture element](https://teo.fdot.gov/architecture/architectures/d1/html/elements/el7.html)). **Punta Gorda contracts its signal maintenance to the county** — stated on the city's Traffic Signals, Signs & Striping Coordination page (ci.punta-gorda.fl.us; page 403s to bots but confirmed live via search snippets). Street lighting: Charlotte County Lighting District (~2,200 roadway lights). No written signal-timing procedures published — request from Public Works. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Development review process & submittal requirements:** [Site Plan Review](https://www.charlottecountyfl.gov/departments/community-development/planning-zoning/site-plan-review) (Community Development, Planning & Zoning) — online application; preliminary then final site plan review, each stage stated ≤ 30 business days. [Site Plan Review Brochure (PDF)](https://www.charlottecountyfl.gov/core/fileparse.php/363/urlt/Site-Plan-Review-Brochure.pdf) and [Preliminary Site Plan Checklist (PDF)](https://www.charlottecountyfl.gov/file/363/Preliminary-Site-Plan-Checklist.pdf). Subdivision requirements: Ch. 3-7 ([Municode](https://library.municode.com/fl/charlotte_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIIILADEGRMA_CH3-7SURE_ARTIVSTIMBECO)). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Permit forms (ROW, driveway):** [Right-of-Way Permit Requirements](https://www.charlottecountyfl.gov/departments/community-development/building-construction/permits/right-of-way-permit.stml) — permits required for all work in county ROW/easements incl. driveway installation/reinstallation/resurfacing, culverts, utilities; submitted at the Murdock permitting office, concurrent with the building permit (standalone ROW permit available for driveway-only work). Detail pages: [Right of Way Permit](https://www.charlottecountyfl.gov/departments/community-development/building-construction/permits/row-permits/right-of-way.stml) · [Residential Driveway](https://www.charlottecountyfl.gov/departments/community-development/building-construction/permits/residential-permits/residential-driveway.stml). (checked 2026-07-08)
