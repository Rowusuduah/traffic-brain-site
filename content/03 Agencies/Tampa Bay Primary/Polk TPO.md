**Role/jurisdiction:** Countywide metropolitan planning organization for Polk County (established 1977; styled "Transportation Planning Organization"), staffed within Polk County government in Bartow — FDOT District 1. Planning agency only: LRTP, TIP, priority projects, UPWP, and safety planning. The TPO does **not** review development, issue permits, or maintain roads — TIAs, driveway/ROW permits, and signal operations belong to [[Polk County]] or the cities ([[City of Lakeland]], Winter Haven, etc.).

**Known starting points** (resolved 2026-07-08 unless tagged):
- **LRTP:** [Envision 2050](https://polktpo.com/plans-and-projects/long-range-transportation-plan/) — adopted by the TPO board **2025-12-09**; [full plan PDF](https://polktpolive.s3.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/12102028/Adopted_LRTP_Envision_2050.pdf). Prior plan [Momentum 2045 (PDF)](https://polktpo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Momentum-2045-LRTP-sm.pdf) archived on the same page.
- **TIP:** [Transportation Improvement Program](https://polktpo.com/plans-and-projects/transportation-improvement-plan/) — current [TIP FY 2025/26–2029/30 (PDF)](https://polktpo.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Transportation_Improvement_Program_FY_2025_26_to_2029_30_adopted_June_12_2025.pdf), adopted 2025-06-12; amendments (latest 2026-04-09) posted on the same page.
- **Priority projects:** [Priority Projects](https://polktpo.com/plans-and-projects/priority-projects/) (annual list to FDOT for the Five-Year Work Program) + [Polk Transportation Projects Guide (PDF)](https://polktpo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Polk-Transportation-Projects-Guide.pdf).
- The TPO's **Roadway Network Database** (annual AADT/PM-peak/LOS report) is the best free traffic-volume source for Polk arterials and collectors — see Collect below.
- Safety umbrella is [Vision Zero Polk](https://polktpo.com/plans-and-projects/vision-zero-polk/); a countywide SS4A Vision Zero Action Plan (Polk County lead, $720k FY2022 SS4A planning grant) is underway.

## Collect (link each as found; date it)
- **TIA/TIS guidelines:** not an MPO function — the TPO publishes no traffic study guidelines. Development traffic studies are governed by the Polk County Land Development Code or the reviewing city — see [[Polk County]] / [[City of Lakeland]]. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Parking study guidelines / parking code (with section #):** not an MPO function — parking standards live in county/city land development codes. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Traffic calming program/guidelines:** not an MPO program — traffic calming requests go to Polk County Roads & Drainage or the relevant city. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Complete Streets policy · Vision Zero / Safety Action Plan:** TPO-adopted **Complete Streets Policy** summarized on the [Complete Streets page](https://polktpo.com/plans-and-projects/vision-zero-polk/complete-streets/) (goals only; the 2012 Complete Street Action Plans for eight high-crash corridors are described but not posted — request from the TPO). [Vision Zero Polk hub](https://polktpo.com/plans-and-projects/vision-zero-polk/) · [Vision Zero Conditions Assessment Study, 2023 (PDF)](https://polktpo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/vision-zero-conditions-assessment-2023.pdf) — the study that supported Polk County's SS4A grant · countywide **Polk Vision Zero Action Plan** in development (two-year SS4A effort; engagement site [polkvisionzero.mysocialpinpoint.com](https://polkvisionzero.mysocialpinpoint.com/)) · **Bicycle and Pedestrian Safety Action Plans** (2016, jointly updated 2020) — [summary page](https://polktpo.com/plans-and-projects/vision-zero-polk/pedestrian-safety-action-plans/) only; the page says to contact the TPO for copies. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Roadway design standards / standard details:** not an MPO function — county/city standards apply (FDOT on state roads). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Access management standards:** not an MPO function — FDOT (state roads) or county/city LDC. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Traffic count requirements (seasonal factors accepted?):** the TPO sets no study requirements, but it is the county's main free **count data source**: [Roadway Network Database](https://polktpo.com/plans-and-projects/highways-and-roads/roadway-network-database/) — ~425 segments (950 directional links) of arterials/collectors with AADT, PM peak-hour (4–7 p.m.) two-way and directional volumes, and LOS A–F. Current edition: [2026 Roadway Network Database (PDF)](https://polktpolive.s3.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/03105920/2026-Roadway-Network-Database.pdf); 2025 and 2023 editions archived on the same page. FDOT Traffic Online covers state roads. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Crash/safety dashboard:** no TPO-hosted crash dashboard found online — crash analysis lives in the Bicycle/Pedestrian Safety Action Plans and the Vision Zero Conditions Assessment Study; the Vision Zero hub also links the community-safety site [polksafety.org](https://www.polksafety.org/). Signal Four Analytics requires agency login. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **GIS / open data portal:** [Maps and Data](https://polktpo.com/resources/maps-and-data/) — no standalone TPO open-data hub; the TPO publishes through the **Polk County GIS portal**: [Polk TPO Roads Map Viewer](https://gis.polk-county.net/portal/home/webmap/viewer.html?layers=909a88bb1e73423cbd5cbdcabfa7d76c) (roads, trails, traffic, population/employment) plus StoryMaps (pedestrian/bicycle safety initiatives; urbanization history). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Signal timing procedures & maintaining agency:** not an MPO function — signals are maintained by Polk County, the cities, or FDOT depending on the road; confirm maintainer per intersection with [[Polk County]] `#status/verify`. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Development review process & submittal requirements:** not an MPO function — the TPO is not a development-review agency; submittals go to Polk County Land Development or the city. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Permit forms (ROW, driveway):** not an MPO function — see [[Polk County]] / city pages. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Example projects (ours & public):** ours live in the private vault (see callout below); public reference material: [Documents library](https://polktpo.com/resources/documents/) and the corridor studies on [Highways and Roads](https://polktpo.com/plans-and-projects/highways-and-roads/) (e.g., Ridge Scenic Highway Master Plan). (checked 2026-07-08)
