**Role/jurisdiction:** Unincorporated county roads only — the county does not review or permit work inside Bradenton, Palmetto, Anna Maria, Holmes Beach, Bradenton Beach, or Longboat Key (town straddles the Manatee/Sarasota line). Heavy development review volume (Parrish, Lakewood Ranch corridor). Land development review runs through the **Development Services Department** (electronic submittal via the Accela Online Services Portal); roadway operations through Public Works **Traffic Management Division**.

**Known starting points** (resolved 2026-07-08 unless tagged):
- The governing traffic study document is now the **Traffic Study Guidelines, March 20, 2026** (Development Services) — it superseded the long-standing March 2015 Public Works guidelines. Transportation **concurrency is still active** here (unlike Hillsborough's mobility fees).
- Traffic calming: the **Neighborhood Traffic Calming Program (NTCP)** was re-adopted 2025-05-06 (Resolution R-25-001) — criteria and petition mechanics below.
- Signals countywide (including Bradenton and Palmetto systems) are run from the **Manatee–Sarasota Regional Traffic Management Center** under a 2005 interlocal — see signals entry.
- Always confirm FDOT vs. County maintenance for a corridor — major arterials (US 41, US 301, SR 64, SR 70) are state roads, **FDOT District 1** `#status/verify` per corridor.

## Collect (link each as found; date it)
- **TIA/TIS guidelines:** [Manatee County Traffic Study Guidelines (signed PDF)](https://www.mymanatee.org/docs/default-source/development-services-department-documents/planning/traffic-study-guidelines-signed.pdf?sfvrsn=3238cf21_3) — Development Services Department, **March 20, 2026 edition** (24 pp.). Study types: Traffic Study Waiver (supported by a trip-generation comparison), Trip Reservation Report/Operational Analysis (TRR/OA) for administratively approved projects, **Multimodal Site Access and Safety Analysis (MSASA)** mandatory for applications heard by the BOCC, and Comprehensive Plan Amendment Traffic Study (CPA-TS). "De Minimis" impact = fewer than 50 gross PM-peak-hour trips **or** fewer than 300 gross daily trips. Pre-study **methodology meeting with Transportation Planning staff** (and the County's traffic review consultant when required) is required. Plain-language overview on the county's [Transportation Planning page](https://www.mymanatee.org/connect/news-and-information/news-and-information/article-detail/planning-development-and-zoning-division-posts/2024/12/27/transportation-planning). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Parking study guidelines / parking code (with section #):** LDC **Chapter 10 — Transportation Management**: **§ 1005 Off-Street Parking** and **§ 1006 Off-Street Loading** ([Municode](https://library.municode.com/fl/manatee_county/codes/land_development_code?nodeId=CH10TRMA)); lot construction per the Public Works Standards. No standalone county parking *study* guideline is published online — reduction/alternative-rate study expectations: request from Development Services. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Traffic calming program/guidelines:** [Neighborhood Traffic Calming Program (PDF)](https://www.mymanatee.org/media/docs/default-source/public-works-department-documents/traffic-management-division-documents/manatee-county-neighborhood-traffic-calming-program.pdf) — adopted **2025-05-06** (Resolution R-25-001, Exhibit A). Key mechanics: public **local residential streets in the unincorporated county only** (thoroughfares excluded); request from an HOA or a group of ≥ 4 separately owned abutting properties; eligibility screen = posted speed ≤ 30 mph, ADT > 150 and ≤ 2,000 vpd, and 85th percentile ≥ 5 mph over posted; a volume/speed/multimodal **grading system needs ≥ 6 points** to proceed; petition needs **67% "yes" of property owners** in the study area **plus** "yes" from owners within 100 ft of each device; requestors **pay the device cost** (county installs/maintains); BOCC approval before installation. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Complete Streets policy · Vision Zero / Safety Action Plan:** county safety plan = [Traffic & Road Safety Program and Action Plan (PDF)](https://www.mymanatee.org/media/docs/default-source/public-works-department-documents/traffic-management-division-documents/traffic-road-safety-program-action-plan.pdf) — Public Works, **June 2025**, developed with federal **SS4A** funding. No standalone county-adopted Complete Streets policy found published online (complete-streets goals were added to the Comprehensive Plan circa 2013) `#status/verify` — request from the county. Regional: Sarasota/Manatee MPO **Destination Zero** [Safety Action Plan](https://www.mympo.org/destination-zero/safety-action-plan) (first adopted 2022-02-28). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Roadway design standards / standard details:** **Manatee County Public Works Standards** — Part 3, **Highway and Traffic Standards Manual (adopted April 2022**, Resolution R22-039**)**, plus transportation/stormwater/utilities parts — all hosted on the [Public Works Standards ArcGIS Hub](https://public-works-standards-public-manateegis.hub.arcgis.com/); landing page: [View Public Works Standards](https://www.mymanatee.org/services-and-amenities/service-listing/service-details/view-public-works-standards). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Access management standards:** LDC Chapter 10: **§ 1000 Right-of-Way Standards, § 1001 Street Standards, § 1004 Driveway Regulations** ([Municode](https://library.municode.com/fl/manatee_county/codes/land_development_code?nodeId=CH10TRMA)); turn-lane and access-spacing design per the Public Works Standards (Part 3). Driveway connections to county roads need an access & drainage permit — see permits entry. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Traffic count requirements (seasonal factors accepted?):** the 2026 Traffic Study Guidelines require analysis of **peak-hour, peak-season** conditions and counts collected **within 12 months** of the application; the word "seasonal factor" is not used — confirm the accepted adjustment source (FDOT vs. county factors) at the methodology meeting `#status/verify`. County count data: **343 locations (20 permanent continuous + ~293 portable), updated annually** — [View Traffic Volume Data](https://www.mymanatee.org/services-and-amenities/service-listing/service-details/view-traffic-volume-data) · [interactive counts map](https://www.mymanatee.org/gisapps/mobile/index.html?type=traffic-counts) · [Traffic Count Stations dataset](https://public-manateegis.opendata.arcgis.com/datasets/traffic-count-stations) on open data. (checked 2026-07-08)

- **Signal timing procedures & maintaining agency:** Public Works Traffic Management runs signals from the **(Manatee–Sarasota) Regional Traffic Management Center** in Bradenton — 200+ signals in Manatee County monitored, ATMS/CCTV/travel-time instrumentation ([county TMC article](https://www.mymanatee.org/connect/news-and-information/news-and-information/article-detail/traffic-management-division-posts/2025/01/09/regional-traffic-management-center)).
