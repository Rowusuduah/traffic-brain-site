**Role/jurisdiction:** Unincorporated Collier County (Naples region) — the county does not review or permit work inside the City of Naples, City of Marco Island, or Everglades City. High-standard review environment; development review runs through Growth Management Community Development (GMCD, electronic submittal via the CityView portal), transportation review through the Transportation Planning Section, roadway operations through Transportation Management Services / Traffic Operations. Note this is **FDOT District 1**, not District 7 like the Tampa Bay core counties.

**Known starting points** (resolved 2026-07-08 unless tagged):
- `collier.gov` returns **403 to non-browser clients** (curl and WebFetch alike). Every collier.gov link below was confirmed live via the current search index and/or Wayback Machine captures (Oct 2025 – Jan 2026) — noted per entry where it matters.
- Access management was **re-adopted in 2025** (Resolution 2025-150, replacing the Res. 13-257 / 01-247 lineage) — do not cite the old policy numbers.
- Collier still runs **transportation concurrency** (checkbook via the Annual Update & Inventory Report, AUIR) rather than mobility fees — different world from Hillsborough `#status/verify` current mechanics per project.
- Regional safety plan is the **Collier MPO's** Comprehensive Safety Action Plan (SS4A), approved 2025-10-10.
- Always confirm FDOT vs. County vs. City of Naples maintenance for a corridor before citing standards (US 41, SR/CR splits are common) `#status/verify` per corridor.

## Collect (link each as found; date it)
- **TIA/TIS guidelines:** [TIS Guidelines — current version (PDF)](https://www.collier.gov/files/assets/county/v/1/transportation/documents/planning-studies/tis_guidelines-current-version.pdf) — "Procedures and Guidelines for Traffic Impact Studies" (created 2003-02-11, revised 2003-11-14; still what the county posts as current) together with [Resolution 2006-299 — TIS Guidelines (PDF)](https://www.collier.gov/files/assets/county/v/1/transportation/documents/planning-studies/resolution-2006-299-tis-guidelines.pdf); both are linked from the Transportation Planning Section page as the governing TIS documents. Key mechanics from the posted guide: complete-TIS warrant at >1,000 ADT or >100 vph peak season; hand trip distribution allowed under 500 peak-hour trips; prior-use trip credit if active within 5 years; HCM-based capacity analysis; methodology set at an initial meeting with staff. (403 to bots; confirmed live via search index + Wayback 2025-10-28.) (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Parking study guidelines / parking code (with section #):** LDC **§ 4.05.00 — Off-Street Parking and Loading**, rates in **§ 4.05.04** ([Collier LDC on Municode](https://library.municode.com/fl/collier_county/codes/land_development_code)). No standalone county parking *study* guideline is published online — expectations for alternative-rate/shared-parking justification: request from GMCD Zoning. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Traffic calming program/guidelines:** Neighborhood Traffic Management Program (NTMP) — [Traffic Calming page](https://www.collier.gov/County-Development/Transportation-Management/Traffic-Operations/Transportation-Planning/Traffic-Calming) and [NTMP Manual, 2019 (PDF)](https://www.collier.gov/files/assets/county/v/1/transportation/documents/ntmp-manual-2019.pdf), run by the Transportation Planning Section. Local/minor-collector residential streets; county collects the traffic data and applies a three-step process (education/enforcement → passive measures → physical devices with financing scenarios), with a resident "Traffic Team" of 3–5 members carrying the neighborhood decisions. (403 to bots; both links confirmed via Wayback capture of the Transportation Planning page, 2025-10-28.) (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Complete Streets policy · Vision Zero / Safety Action Plan:** no county-adopted Complete Streets policy or county Vision Zero plan found published online `#status/verify` — request from the county. The operative regional document is the **Collier MPO Comprehensive Safety Action Plan** (SS4A; covers the county plus Naples, Marco Island, Everglades City), approved at the 2025-10-10 MPO Board meeting — [final plan (PDF)](https://colliermpo.org/wp-content/uploads/CollierSAP_Final10242025v2approved_FINAL_DOCUMENT.pdf) and appendices on the [MPO Traffic Safety page](https://colliermpo.org/other-programs-documents/traffic-safety/). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Roadway design standards / standard details:** [Construction Standards Handbook for Work Within the Public Right-of-Way, 2016 edition (PDF)](https://www.collier.gov/files/assets/county/v/3/growth-management/documents/right-of-way/2016-row-handbook.pdf) — adopted by Resolution 2016-136; the ROW Permitting page still lists it as current ("last updated 2016"), with FDOT standards incorporated by reference. Companion: [MOT Policy (PDF)](https://www.collier.gov/files/assets/county/v/1/growth-management/documents/right-of-way/mot-policy.pdf). In the LDC: **§ 4.04.00 — Transportation System Standards** ([Municode](https://library.municode.com/fl/collier_county/codes/land_development_code)). (403 to bots; confirmed via Wayback capture of the ROW Permitting page, 2026-01-17.) (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Access management standards:** **Access Management Policy, Resolution 2025-150** ([PDF](https://www.collier.gov/files/assets/county/v/1/transportation/documents/planning-studies/resolution-2025-150-access-management.pdf)) — maintained by the Transportation Planning Section; supersedes the earlier Res. 13-257 policy. Driveway/connection design specifics also in the 2016 ROW Handbook (e.g., one driveway for frontages under 100 ft). (403 to bots; confirmed via Wayback capture of the Transportation Planning page, 2025-10-28.) (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Traffic count requirements (seasonal factors accepted?):** Collier publishes its own count program — [Traffic Counts page](https://www.collier.gov/County-Development/Transportation-Management/Traffic-Operations/Traffic-Counts) with quarterly/annual Traffic Count Report PDFs from 2005 through 2025 Q1–Q2 (ADT, both directions). Collier is a strongly seasonal county and the TIS warrant thresholds are expressed per *peak season* — confirm current peak-season adjustment expectations with Transportation Planning before submitting `#status/verify`. (Page 403 to bots; confirmed via Wayback 2025-10-28.) (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Crash/safety dashboard:** none published by the county itself — no county crash dashboard found online; request data from the county, or use the MPO SAP's [Appendix B — Existing Conditions & Safety Analysis (PDF)](https://colliermpo.org/wp-content/uploads/Appendix-B_Existing-Conditions-Safety-Analysis.pdf) (Signal Four Analytics requires agency login). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **GIS / open data portal:** [Collier County GIS Hub (ArcGIS Hub)](https://hub-collierbcc.opendata.arcgis.com/) — countywide open data (CSV/KML/GeoJSON/shapefile + API); department hub for development data: [GMCD GIS Hub](https://gis-colliercountygmd.hub.arcgis.com/). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Signal timing procedures & maintaining agency:** Traffic Operations runs the signal system and a **Traffic Management Center** (continuous monitoring weekdays 6:30 am–6:00 pm; signal issues 239-252-5000). Per the FDOT District 1 [Regional ITS Architecture stakeholder entry](https://teo.fdot.gov/architecture/architectures/d1/html/stakeholders/sh38.html), the county operates/maintains ~283 signals in the unincorporated county while the **City of Naples operates its own 42 signals** and Marco Island its own system — the architecture data may lag, so verify the maintaining agency per corridor `#status/verify`. No written signal-timing procedures document published online — request from Traffic Operations. (County page 403 to bots; confirmed via Wayback 2025-10-28.) (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Development review process & submittal requirements:** GMCD electronic submittal and record search via the [CityView portal](https://cvportal.colliercountyfl.gov/CityViewWeb/) (portal drops non-browser connections; confirmed live via search index) with the county's [How to Use the GMCD Portal](https://www.collier.gov/County-Development/Operations-Regulatory-Management/How-to-Use-the-GMCD-Portal) guide; pre-application meetings are tracked as PL numbers in the same system. TIS methodology sign-off comes through Transportation Planning within the GMCD review. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Permit forms (ROW, driveway):** [Right-of-Way (ROW) Permitting](https://www.collier.gov/County-Development/Land-Development/Right-of-Way-Permitting) — permit required for any construction in public ROW/easements; page hosts the ROW application, fee schedule (2017), 2016 ROW Handbook, MOT Policy, and the list of roadways requiring a ROW permit. (403 to bots; confirmed via Wayback 2026-01-17.) (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Example projects (ours & public):** ours live in the private vault (see callout below). Public TIS examples are unusually easy here — submitted Traffic Impact Statements are retrievable as PDFs from planning records on the public CityView portal (search by PL number). (checked 2026-07-08)
