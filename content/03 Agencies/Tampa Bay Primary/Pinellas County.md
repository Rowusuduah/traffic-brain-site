**Role/jurisdiction:** Densest FL county. Public Works owns county-maintained roads (unincorporated plus county arterials/collectors that run through the 24 municipalities) — and note the county's access standards apply to development on county-maintained right-of-way **even inside a municipality** (TDM § 5.1). Countywide land-use + MPO coordination runs through [[Forward Pinellas]] (unique dual planning council/MPO role), which also owns the countywide safety plan, count maps, and the multimodal impact fee framework.

**Known starting points** (resolved 2026-07-08 unless tagged):
- The single central document is the **Transportation Design Manual (Jan 2021)** — it bundles traffic study guidelines, Complete Streets, access management, roadway/signal design, retiming, and calming into one 48-page manual.
- Traffic calming confirmed: the **Residential Traffic Management Program** (BOCC-adopted Oct 1997) exists, but the program document itself is not posted online — see below.
- GIS confirmed well-developed: Enterprise GIS open-data hub + county app gallery (parcels, road centerlines, bike facilities, impact-fee districts).
- Signals: the county runs a countywide interconnected system; **St. Petersburg and Clearwater operate their own traffic control centers** — see signal entry below.
- Transportation concurrency is gone countywide — replaced by the Forward Pinellas **Mobility Plan / Multimodal Impact Fee** system (LDC Ch. 150 Art. II); ordinance update in progress through ~June 2026.
- Many major arterials (US 19, Gulf-to-Bay, Alt US 19…) are FDOT District 7 roads — always confirm state vs. county maintenance per corridor `#status/verify`.

## Collect (link each as found; date it)
- **TIA/TIS guidelines:** [Transportation Design Manual, January 2021 (PDF)](https://pinellas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/TransDesignManual.pdf) — **Chapter 3, Traffic Study Guidelines**. Key mechanics: a **methodology letter** must be prepared and **signed by the project's traffic engineer and the County Transportation Division before the study starts** (study area, scope, count types, and the acceptable time of year / days of week for data collection are fixed in the letter); county consults the city PW department for projects on city roads or within 1/4 mile of an incorporated area, and FDOT for state roads. Ch. 3 enumerates study types: traffic analysis, traffic impact study, signal warrant, **ICE (required whenever alternative intersection improvements are considered)**, gap, intersection delay, travel time/delay, no-passing zone, advisory speed, spot speed, and transportation safety study (safety B/C per FDM 122). References are FDOT handbooks, HCM, ITE Trip Generation. No separate standalone TIS manual exists — Ch. 3 is it. Fee side: [Multimodal Impact Fees, LDC Ch. 150 Art. II](https://library.municode.com/fl/pinellas_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIIILADECO_CH150IMFE_ARTIIMUIMFE) under the countywide Mobility Plan; [MIFO update](https://forwardpinellas.org/projects/multimodal-impact-fee-ordinance/) (Forward Pinellas + County + St. Pete + Clearwater + Largo) underway, Phase 2A runs to ~June 2026. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Parking study guidelines / parking code (with section #):** unincorporated LDC — **Ch. 138 Zoning, Art. X Community Design Standards, Div. 2 Parking and Loading, § 138-3602 Motor Vehicle Parking** ([Municode Art. X](https://library.municode.com/fl/pinellas_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIIILADECO_CH138ZO_ARTXCODEST)); stall quantity minimums/limits per use in **Table 138-3602.a**. No standalone county parking *study* guideline is published online — reduction/shared-parking study expectations: request from Development Review Services. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Traffic calming program/guidelines:** **Residential Traffic Management Program (RTMP)** — adopted by the BOCC **October 1997** (confirmed in [Comp Plan Transportation Element, Ch. 7 "Safety, Efficiency and Goods Movement" (PDF)](https://plan.pinellas.gov/comp_plan/03trans/ch-7.pdf)). TDM **Ch. 16** designates the RTMP document as the county's calming methods reference for all county roads, but says to obtain a copy by **contacting Public Works Traffic Engineering — the program document is not published online**. Criteria flavor from the Comp Plan: speeds **< 12 mph over posted** are treated as minor (enforcement, speed-limit review, education); excessive speeding triggers physical measures (speed humps, roundabouts, street/intersection narrowing, non-local traffic diversion). Request the current handbook + petition thresholds from the agency. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Complete Streets policy · Vision Zero / Safety Action Plan:** TDM **Ch. 4 Complete Streets** — tiered corridor prioritization from the county's Complete Streets Corridor Evaluation Report (Tier 1 = overlap of Forward Pinellas Active Transportation Plan + County Paving Plan; corridors > 45 mph posted and > 25,000 AADT excluded). Countywide grants/program: [Forward Pinellas Complete Streets](https://forwardpinellas.org/complete-streets/). Vision Zero is **Forward Pinellas's**, not the county's: [Safe Streets Pinellas](https://forwardpinellas.org/safestreets/) — [Action Plan](https://forwardpinellas.org/safestreets/the-action-plan/) adopted 2021, zero deaths/serious injuries by 2045; [High-Injury Network (PDF)](https://forwardpinellas.org/safestreets/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/6-Safe-Streets-Pinellas-Action-Plan-High-Injury-Network.pdf) (HIN ≈ 4% of network carries ~60% of KSI). SS4A-funded [plan update](https://forwardpinellas.org/pinellas/safe-streets-update/) in progress (community workshops spring 2026; public crash dashboard promised). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Roadway design standards / standard details:** hub page: [Engineering & Technical Information](https://pinellas.gov/engineering-technical-information-public-works-and-utilities/). Governing docs: **Transportation Design Manual (Jan 2021)** — Ch. 9 roadway design (design speeds, lane widths, turn lanes, return radii, roundabouts), Ch. 10 pavement, Ch. 11–13 MOT/signs/markings, Ch. 17 lighting; **[Standard Engineering Details, Feb 2022 (PDF)](https://pinellas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/pc-standard-details-feb-2022.pdf)**; **Standard Technical Specifications for Roadway & Related Construction** — current **January 2025** edition ([2025 Supplemental Specifications PDF](https://pinellas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/2025-Pinellas-County-Supplemental-Specifications.pdf); [July 2019 base specs](https://pinellas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/roadway-tech-specs-July-2019.pdf) archived on the hub page). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Access management standards:** LDC **Ch. 154, Art. IV — Access Management** ([Municode](https://library.municode.com/fl/pinellas_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIIILADECO_CH154SIDERI-WIMSUPL_ARTIVACMA)) plus TDM **Ch. 5** (the working version with tables). Key numbers: driveway connection spacing by road class — Class 1 controlled access; Class 2 > 680 ft; Class 3 > 460 ft; Class 4 > 240 ft; Class 5 > 120 ft; Class 6 > 0 ft (Table 5-1); median opening spacing 1,320/990/660/330 ft for Classes 1–4 (Table 5-2). Additional access to an arterial/collector may be permitted when the project generates **> 555 daily trips**, backed by a county-approved traffic report. Compliance applies to all new development/redevelopment/changes of use on or adjacent to county-maintained ROW, **whether or not the site is inside a municipality**. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Traffic count requirements (seasonal factors accepted?):** no published blanket seasonal-factor policy — the TDM Ch. 3 methodology letter explicitly fixes **time of year and days of week** for counts per study, so seasonal treatment is negotiated per project. Published count data: [Forward Pinellas Traffic Count Maps](https://forwardpinellas.org/document-portal/traffic-count-maps/) — annual countywide AADT map PDFs, **2014 through 2024**; no interactive count database found (a 2023 county RFP for a "Crash Data, Traffic Counts and Level of Service Database Program" suggests one is coming `#status/verify`). FDOT Florida Traffic Online covers state roads. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Crash/safety dashboard:** no county-published crash dashboard. Closest public tools: [Bicycle–Pedestrian Crash Analysis story map](https://pinellas-egis.maps.arcgis.com/apps/MapJournal/index.html?appid=c51a0238936d4b84883180c1b9b44ed7) (county Enterprise GIS) and the Safe Streets Pinellas collision assessment/HIN PDFs; a public-facing crash dashboard is a stated deliverable of the SS4A Safe Streets update. Signal Four Analytics requires an agency login. (checked 2026-07-08)
- **GIS / open data portal:** [Pinellas County Enterprise GIS open data hub](https://new-pinellas-egis.opendata.arcgis.com/) (parcels, road centerlines, zoning, bike facilities, Multi-Modal Impact Fee districts) · [county GIS app gallery](https://egis.pinellas.gov/apps/egis/apps.html) (road closures, pavement program, crash story map) · landing: [Find Maps (GIS)](https://pinellas.gov/services/maps-gis/). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Signal timing procedures & maintaining agency:** county Public Works runs the countywide **ATMS ("Smart Tracs") and Traffic Management Center** at the Clearwater PW campus, staffed 5:30 a.m.–7 p.m. daily — [Smart Tracs Transportation System](https://pinellas.gov/smarts-tracs-transportation-system/). Signal shop operations (signals, flashers, street lighting) are performed **countywide under County Charter responsibilities and interlocal agreements with municipalities** — most small cities' signals are county-maintained; **St. Petersburg and Clearwater operate their own traffic control centers**, coordinated with the county TMC (per the [FDOT D7 ITS architecture entry](https://teo.fdot.gov/architecture/architectures/d7/html/elements/el103.html)). Retiming procedure: TDM **Ch. 15** — get existing timings from the county Traffic Control Center, deliver all analyses/timings as **Synchro** electronic files, clearance intervals per FDOT criteria; signal design per TDM Ch. 14 + Pinellas Standard Details. New-signal requests: [online request form](https://pinellas.gov/services/request-a-new-sign-or-signal/). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Development review process & submittal requirements:** [Development Review topic page](https://pinellas.gov/topic/building-development/development-review/) — Building & Development Review Services covers unincorporated county (plus six partner communities for building services); electronic submittal via the [Pinellas County Access Portal (Accela)](https://aca-prod.accela.com/pinellas/Default.aspx). Access management and traffic study requirements are applied through the site plan review process (Comp Plan Transp. Element Ch. 7). Example checklist: [DRS Plat Submittal Checklist (PDF)](https://pinellas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/DRS-Submittal-Checklist.pdf). (checked 2026-07-08)
- **Permit forms (ROW, driveway):** [Right-of-Way Utilization Permit](https://pinellas.gov/services/apply-for-a-right-of-way-utilization-permit/) — required for private work in county ROW including **new/replacement driveways**, sidewalks, utility connections, temporary access; apply through the Access Portal (Development → Right of Way under Development Review Services); [application form (PDF)](https://pinellas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/rowuseapp.pdf); Public Works must be notified 48 hours before starting work or the permit is voided. (checked 2026-07-08)
