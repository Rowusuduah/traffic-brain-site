# CMF Clearinghouse
**What:** FHWA-funded searchable database of Crash Modification Factors with star-quality ratings.
**Why:** the citable source for "this countermeasure reduces crashes by X%"; star rating + applicability (crash type, severity, context) decide defensibility.
**When:** countermeasure selection, safety B/C, calming effectiveness claims.
**Example questions:** CMF for adding a raised median on 4-lane urban arterial, for KABC injury crashes? Is the study context comparable to mine?
**Rule:** never quote a CMF without its star rating, crash type, and base condition.
