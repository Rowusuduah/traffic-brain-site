**What:** BOE Manual defines pay items (XXX-XX-XXX structure), units, and what each includes; historical cost data available for estimating.
**Why:** quantities are only meaningful attached to correct items; "included in cost of other items" answers half of quantity questions.
**When:** quantity takeoffs, engineer's estimates, tab sheet reviews.
