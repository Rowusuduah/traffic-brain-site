# Highway Capacity Manual (HCM)
**What:** TRB's methodologies for capacity/LOS — signalized & unsignalized intersections, segments, freeways, multimodal.
**Why:** every LOS number in a TIA traces to an HCM method; Synchro/HCS implement it and their settings ARE HCM inputs.
**When:** LOS analysis, interpreting delay thresholds (signalized A≤10s … F>80s; unsignalized F>50s — verify table), choosing method when software results diverge.
**Example questions:** why do Synchro "Percentile" and HCM delays differ? when is HCM unsignalized method invalid (e.g., near signals)?
