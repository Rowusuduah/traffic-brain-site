TRB's methodologies for capacity/LOS — signalized & unsignalized intersections, segments, freeways, multimodal.

**Why:** every LOS number in a TIA traces to an HCM method; Synchro/HCS implement it and their settings ARE HCM inputs.

**When:**
- LOS analysis
- interpreting delay thresholds (signalized A≤10s … F>80s; unsignalized F>50s)
- choosing method when software results diverge

> [!warning] Verify the delay threshold table against the current edition before citing.

**Example questions:**
- why do Synchro "Percentile" and HCM delays differ?
- when is HCM unsignalized method invalid (e.g., near signals)?
