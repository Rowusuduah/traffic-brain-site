**What:** federal Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices — signs, markings, signals: design, placement, warrants. Florida adopts per s.316.0745 F.S.; FDOT supplements via [[FDOT Traffic Engineering Manual (TEM)]].
**Why:** legal standard for every TCD; warrants (signals Ch. 4C, multiway stop, ped signals) come from here.
**When:** signal warrant analyses, sign/marking design & review, school zones, TTC principles (Part 6, implemented in FL via Standard Plans 102-series).
**Example questions:** which of the 9 signal warrants might apply and what data does each need? advance warning sign spacing? when is a marked crosswalk appropriate?
**Spawn:** warrant-by-warrant data checklist · [[SPM Plan Review Checklist]] feeds.
**Interim Approvals** (e.g., RRFB history) live on the FHWA site — check IA list for newer devices.
**Free companions:** [11th Edition official page](https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/kno_11th_Edition.htm) — full free PDF, the 2009→11th Edition redline, summary of changes, known-errors list, hotlinked navigation version, and two recorded FHWA webinars.

> [!tip] Revision 1 (Dec 2025) is out. Before citing a section, confirm you're reading the revised text; and when a reviewer learned on the 2009 edition, the redline is the fastest way to show exactly what changed.
