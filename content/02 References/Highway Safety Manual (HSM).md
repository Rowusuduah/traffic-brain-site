**What:** quantitative safety: SPFs (predicted crashes), Empirical Bayes (expected), CMFs to compare alternatives.
**Why:** moves you from "crashes happened" to "how many are expected, and what does treatment X buy?" — the language of HSIP funding and serious safety studies.
**When:** network screening, countermeasure B/C, before-after framing, safety sections in alternatives analyses.
**Example questions:** predicted vs expected vs observed? why EB-adjust before claiming a site is "high crash"?
**Free companions:** [FHWA HSM Part C spreadsheets](https://highways.dot.gov/safety/data-analysis-tools/rsdp/rsdp-tools/highway-safety-manual-part-c-spreadsheets) — free Excel tools that run the Part C predictive methods (predicted/expected crash frequency, EB, PSI) for rural two-lane, rural multilane, and urban/suburban arterials. The book is paywalled; this is how you actually run HSM analysis without buying software.
