Trip rates/equations by land use code from national studies; the default demand basis for TIAs (unless agency dictates otherwise).

**Why:** trip gen drives study scope and mitigation; misuse (wrong LU code, rate vs equation, wrong setting/period) is the most-reviewed part of any TIA.

**When:** every TIA; internal capture & pass-by adjustments via the companion Handbook procedures.

**Example questions:**
- rate or equation for this size?
- weekday PM vs peak-of-generator?
- which pass-by % is defensible and what's the agency cap?
