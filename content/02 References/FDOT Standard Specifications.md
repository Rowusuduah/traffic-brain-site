# FDOT Standard Specifications
**What:** contract-language companion to Standard Plans — materials, methods, measurement & payment per section (Div II/III).
**Why:** measurement & payment sections define what a pay item includes → quantity/BOE questions resolve here.
**When:** quantity disputes, "is X incidental?", spec section callouts in plans.
**Example questions:** what does 711 painted markings payment include? sod incidental to what?
