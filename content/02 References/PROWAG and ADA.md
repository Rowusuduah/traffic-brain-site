# PROWAG / ADA Accessibility
**What:** Public Right-of-Way Accessibility Guidelines (final rule 2023 — **verify current DOJ/USDOT adoption status**) + ADA standards as implemented in FDM and Standard Plans (curb ramps 522-002, detectable warnings, APS).
**Why:** accessibility errors are non-negotiable review failures and legal exposure; every ramp, crosswalk, signal ped feature is checked.
**When:** ramp design/review, cross slopes (≤2%), running slopes, landing sizes, ped signal reach/APS, parking ADA counts & routes.
**Example questions:** max ramp running slope & counter slope? required ADA stalls for 240-space lot and where do they go (access aisle, route)?
