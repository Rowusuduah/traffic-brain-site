Public Right-of-Way Accessibility Guidelines (final rule 2023) + ADA standards as implemented in FDM and Standard Plans (curb ramps 522-002, detectable warnings, APS).

> [!warning] Verify current DOJ/USDOT adoption status of the PROWAG final rule.

**Why:** accessibility errors are non-negotiable review failures and legal exposure; every ramp, crosswalk, signal ped feature is checked.

**When:**
- ramp design/review
- cross slopes (≤2%)
- running slopes
- landing sizes
- ped signal reach/APS
- parking ADA counts & routes

**Example questions:**
- max ramp running slope & counter slope?
- required ADA stalls for 240-space lot and where do they go (access aisle, route)?

> [!tip] Free companions
> [PROWAG final rule full text](https://www.access-board.gov/prowag/) — the actual scoping and technical chapters (curb ramps, APS, crosswalks, on-street parking), which is what you cite in review comments, not the homepage. USDOT adopted PROWAG for transit stops Dec 2024 — the adoption-status question above is partly answered there.
