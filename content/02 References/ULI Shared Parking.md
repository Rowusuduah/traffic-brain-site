# ULI Shared Parking
**What:** methodology + factors for mixed-use sites where uses peak at different times → shared demand < sum of individual peaks. Hourly/monthly profiles, modal & non-captive adjustments.
**Why:** THE defensible basis for shared-parking reductions; many codes reference it by name.
**When:** any mixed-use parking analysis or shared-use agreement justification.
**Example questions:** office+restaurant+residential — governing hour and month? how is non-captive applied without double-counting internal capture?
