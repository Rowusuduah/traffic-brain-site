**What:** the drawings — standard details referenced by index number in construction plans (e.g., 700-series signing, 711 markings, 102-series temporary traffic control, 522-002 curb ramps, 6xx signals).
**Why:** plans say "per Index 711-001"; you must be able to open the right index instantly. Quantities and reviews hinge on these details.
**When:** S&PM design/review, MOT plans, ADA ramps, signal hardware details, quantity takeoffs.
**Example questions:** stop bar placement/size? sign post embedment? lane-closure taper per 102-603? detectable warning placement?
**Spawn:** an index cheat-sheet note per plan type you touch ([[SPM Plan Review Checklist]] links the common ones).
