*This folder runs the machine: the routines, checklists, and trackers that keep the rest of the vault alive and honest.*

- [[How This Vault Works]] — folder structure, naming conventions, the three-axis tagging system, and the linking philosophy.
- [[Daily Capture Routine]] — the ≤5-min habit: open daily note, type one line, back to work in under 15 seconds.
- [[Weekly Review Routine]] — Friday's 20–30 min sweep: promote captures to real notes, batch questions, hit inbox zero.
- [[Monthly Technical Growth Review]] — honest skill stock-take, one gap picked per month, quarterly standards re-check.
- [[Lessons Learned System]] — three-level lesson capture: daily one-liner, per-project section, monthly pattern promotion.
- [[QAQC System]] — three-layer QA/QC: self-check every deliverable, domain checklists, senior review made easy.
- [[Questions for Senior Engineers]] — batched questions that pass the 3-source test before they're asked.
- [[Skill Tracker]] — Know / Learning / Need columns, moved monthly; "did it unassisted on a project" = Know.
- [[Standards Update Tracker]] — the quarterly re-check table for FDM, Standard Plans, TEM, Greenbook, and the rest.
- [[Verification Queue]] — every `#status/verify` claim in the vault, tiered by how badly an error would bite; work it top down.
- [[Plans - 30 to 90 to 3 Years]] — outcome-based horizons, reviewed at the monthly review.
- [[Career Roadmap - EIT to Mastery]] — the five-stage trajectory from reliable producer to PE who owns work.
