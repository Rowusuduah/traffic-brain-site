# Plans — 30 Days → 3 Years

Each horizon has **outcomes**, not activities. Review at [[Monthly Technical Growth Review]].

## First 30 days — wire the system, map the terrain
- [ ] Vault live; daily capture habitual (14-day streak); first [[Weekly Review Routine]] done ×4.
- [ ] Collect the **priority reference set** (see [[MOC - Core References]] → "Collect first") into `02 References` with real links, `last-checked` dated.
- [ ] Fill agency notes for your 3 most active jurisdictions ([[MOC - Agencies]] checklist).
- [ ] Shadow one full deliverable cycle; log every acronym and correction.
- [ ] Meet the seniors: who owns Synchro? design? safety? parking? Note it in [[MOC - Agencies]]-style contact notes.
- [ ] Life admin done once, properly: benefits elections, 401(k) at least to match, direct deposit split to savings ([[Money Basics for a New Engineer]]).

## 90 days — first competence proofs
- [ ] Own one analysis end-to-end under supervision (TIA chain, safety study, or parking study) with your assumptions table and Layer-1 QA/QC attached.
- [ ] [[Synchro QAQC Checklist]] used on real files; found ≥3 of your own errors before review did.
- [ ] Crash pull → summary → draft countermeasures for one corridor ([[How to Pull Crashes from Signal Four Analytics]]).
- [ ] One automation win shipped to the team (chart generator, count cleaner — see [[Starter Automation Projects]]).
- [ ] Question habit visible: seniors see homework attached to every ask.

## 6 months — trusted with pieces of everything
- [ ] Drafted full report sections accepted with light edits ([[MOC - Report Writing]]).
- [ ] Read plans fluently: S&PM, signal, MOT sheets reviewed with a checklist and real comments.
- [ ] ULI Shared Parking analysis built and defended in review ([[ULI Shared Parking Calculation]]).
- [ ] Comment-response matrix run solo through one review cycle.
- [ ] FE→PE plan set: confirmed FBPE early-exam eligibility `#status/verify`, exam date targeted, references list started.
- [ ] Emergency fund ≥1 month expenses; typing ≥55 WPM ([[Professional Habits and Typing]]).

## 1 year — reliable producer, visible trajectory
- [ ] Redline density measurably down (keep the count; data beats feelings).
- [ ] Two domains at "Learning→Know" depth in [[Skill Tracker]]; one internal lunch-and-learn taught.
- [ ] ITE district involvement: attended regularly, volunteered once (FLPRITE network compounds).
- [ ] [[Experience Tracker for PE-PTOE]] has 12 months of decision-grade entries.
- [ ] Vault at ~150+ notes with real project cross-links — retrieval under deadline actually works.

## 3 years — approaching responsible charge
- [ ] PE exam passed; experience clock on schedule (4 yrs post-degree in FL `#status/verify`).
- [ ] Scoping small studies (hours, data plan, fee input) with PE sign-off.
- [ ] QC'ing junior work using the checklists this vault bred.
- [ ] A specialty forming (ops, safety, parking, design, or modeling) + PTOE/RSP decision made ([[PE PTOE RSP Roadmaps]]).
- [ ] Client/agency contacts who ask for *you* by name.
