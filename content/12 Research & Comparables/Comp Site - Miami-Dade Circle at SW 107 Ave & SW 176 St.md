## Location & context
**Site:** SW 107 Ave & SW 176 St, unincorporated Miami-Dade County (South Miami Heights area, Miami-Dade Commission District 9 per the county project listing). · **Roadway type:** local residential grid streets, 2 lanes (1/dir). · **Posted speed:** not published in project sources — confirm in field or from county speed-zone records. · **Context:** single-family residential grid west of US-1/Cutler Bay — confirm land-use detail from current aerial before using in a deliverable.
**Problem being solved:** intersection operations/safety on a residential grid — the county lists the project as "Intersection Improvements"; the specific triggering study (speeds, crash pattern, petition) is not public — request from DTPW Traffic Engineering if the memo needs it.

## Program & policy basis
- Miami-Dade DTPW Traffic Engineering runs the countywide program: it investigates "hazardous traffic conditions" and makes "recommendations for the installation of traffic control and calming devices countywide" — [DTPW Traffic Engineering page](https://www.miamidade.gov/global/transportation/public-works/traffic-engineering.page) (checked 2026-07-08).
- Traffic circles are an explicitly named device in the county's traffic-calming toolbox: the 2025 County–City of Miami interlocal amendment (Legislative Matter 250368) lists "traffic circles, speed humps, historic wayfinding signs, raised intersections, speed tables, speed cushions, chicanes, and mid-block raised crosswalks" as the traffic-calming devices covered (checked 2026-07-08 — see Sources).
- The program is still actively building circles: a June 2026 county solicitation (No. 20250181, MCC 7360) covers traffic circle construction at N Miami Ave & NW 163rd St (checked 2026-07-08 — see Sources).

## What was built
- **Treatment:** new **traffic circle** at the intersection. Official scope (verbatim, DTPW weekly construction report): "clearing and grubbing, drainage installation, construction of Traffic Circle, installation of curb and gutter and sidewalks, milling and resurfacing, adjustment of valves, installation of pavement markings, signage, and sodding." — Project No. 20200231 WO #4, Commission District 9.
- **Approximate year:** under construction by July 2022 (report anticipated completion August 2022); later reports in the same series (Oct 2022) pushed anticipated completion to December 2023. Exact acceptance date not published — **date the build from historical aerials (FDOT APLUS / Google Earth slider)** per [[Workflow - Comparable Sites Research]].
- **Circle diameter, splitter/approach geometry, YIELD signing layout:** not in public sources — **measure from aerial / request county standard detail from DTPW**. Do NOT quote dimensions until then.
- Same report series documents sibling installs, useful for a comp table: retrofit of an **existing** circle at SW 97 Ave & SW 120 St (Project No. 20200255-R, District 8 — geometry fix of NE corner, new ped crossing, two warning flashers, lighting) and a new circle at NW 11 St & NW 30 Ave (Project No. 20200031 WO #1, District 5).
