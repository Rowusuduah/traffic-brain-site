## When a marked crosswalk alone is not enough
Paint-only crosswalks on higher-speed, multilane roads can be worse than nothing — the multiple-threat crash (one car yields, the next lane doesn't) is the classic failure. FHWA's STEP research is the citation: as speed, lane count, and volume rise, the crossing needs escalating treatment, not just fresh thermoplastic. On anything beyond a 2-lane low-speed street, run the selection logic before recommending "install crosswalk."

## STEP selection logic
The **Guide for Improving Pedestrian Safety at Uncontrolled Crossing Locations** (FHWA STEP program) has the selection matrix. Three inputs, one escalating output menu:
1. **Posted speed** — the dominant variable; higher speed pushes you toward PHB/signal territory.
2. **Number of lanes** (and whether a raised median exists) — more lanes crossed without refuge = multiple-threat exposure.
3. **Volume (AADT)** — higher volume = fewer gaps, more driver-yielding failure.

Menu, roughly cheapest→heaviest: high-visibility crosswalk + advance signs/yield markings + lighting → **raised crossing island / median refuge** (halves the crossing problem; pairs with everything) → **RRFB** → **PHB** → **full pedestrian signal** (only where MUTCD signal warrants are met) → **road diet** (4→3) as the geometric fix that makes the cheaper devices viable again.

> [!warning]
> Do not quote speed/lane/AADT breakpoints from memory — pull the current STEP matrix tables AND check FDOT TEM guidance before naming a device in a report. `#status/verify` current STEP tables and TEM section for enhanced/midblock crossings (FDOT adds state-specific criteria and district review steps on the SHS).

## Device notes
- **RRFB** — rapid-flashing warning beacon supplementing crossing signs; pedestrian-actuated; drivers still yield under normal rules. Now codified in **MUTCD 11th Edition, Part 4** (formerly FHWA Interim Approval) `#status/verify` chapter number in current edition (Rev 1, Dec 2025).
- **PHB (pedestrian hybrid beacon)** — dark until actuated, then yellow→red; legally stops traffic. The step up when speed/lanes/volume exceed RRFB territory. Also governed by **MUTCD 11th Edition, Part 4** `#status/verify` chapter + placement criteria.
- **Full signal** — requires an MUTCD pedestrian signal warrant analysis; don't promise one because a resident asked.
- **Refuge island / road diet** — geometric companions, both FHWA Proven Safety Countermeasures; often the real answer when devices alone can't overcome exposure.

> [!tip]
> Get the matrix and per-device tech sheets free from the [STEP resources page](https://highways.dot.gov/safety/pedestrian-bicyclist/step/resources); [PEDSAFE](https://pedbikesafe.org/) walks the same guide interactively from crash type inputs.

## Write-up habit
State inputs (posted speed, lanes, median, AADT), state the matrix cell, state the recommendation, cite the guide edition. One paragraph, defensible.
