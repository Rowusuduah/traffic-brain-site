**Logic chain (never skip a link):** documented crash pattern → contributing factors → candidate treatments targeting THAT pattern → effectiveness (CMF w/ star rating, crash type, base condition) → feasibility/cost → recommendation.
**Selection sources:** [[FHWA Resources]] Proven Safety Countermeasures · PEDSAFE/BIKESAFE selectors · [[CMF Clearinghouse]] · NCHRP syntheses.
**Free companions:** [FHWA Proven Safety Countermeasures](https://highways.dot.gov/safety/proven-safety-countermeasures) — the canonical starting menu: 28 countermeasures, each with its own page (effectiveness/CRFs, context, cost), a filter tool, and a free booklet. Start every pattern→treatment search here before touching the Clearinghouse.
**CMF math:** expected crashes × CMF = after; multiple CMFs multiply only if independent (be conservative; agencies often cap combined reduction) `#status/verify` local policy.
**B/C sketch:** annual benefit = Σ(reduced crashes by severity × crash cost figures — use FDOT/agency-accepted values `#status/verify`); compare to annualized cost.
**Write-up pattern:** "Pattern X (n=, % of total, K+A=) is addressed by [treatment], an FHWA Proven Safety Countermeasure with CMF ___ (★★★★, [crash type]) [cite ID]."
**Practice question:** corridor is 60% rear-end at signals — why is a raised median the wrong first answer, and what's a better-matched menu?
**QA/QC reminder:** every CMF quoted has ID, star rating, crash type, and comparable context — or it's out.
