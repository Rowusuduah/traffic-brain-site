## The statute in four lines
Per **s.316.1895 F.S.** `#status/verify` current statute text:
- School zone speed limit: **not less than 15 mph** except by local regulation; **not more than 20 mph in an urbanized area**.
- In effect only **30 minutes before, during, and 30 minutes after** pupil arrival (including a regularly scheduled breakfast program) and dismissal — not all day.
- Signs must display the times of enforcement **or** use flashing beacons (time clock, automatic device, or manually activated); portable signs only during arrival/dismissal hours.
- Zones established after July 1, 2008 need the **"Speeding Fines Doubled"** notice. Violations per ch. 318.

> [!warning]
> Beacon operation windows are the classic failure: they must match THIS school's actual bell schedule, including early-release days — a beacon flashing at the wrong time undermines enforceability. Get the schedule in writing from the district.

## Where the design details live
- **Signing & marking:** FDOT Standard Plans school-series indexes (school zone signs, markings, crosswalks) `#status/verify` current index numbers in this year's Standard Plans.
- **Operations/process:** FDOT TEM has school zone guidance (zone limits, beacon operation, agency coordination) `#status/verify` TEM chapter.
- School-zone speed-detection (camera) enforcement is authorized by separate, newer statute — flag it, don't cite it from memory `#status/verify`.

## Review checklist
- [ ] Zone speed and limits consistent with s.316.1895 (15–20 mph band, urbanized-area cap)?
- [ ] Sign legend times match the current bell schedule, or beacons provided?
- [ ] Beacon programming covers breakfast-program arrival + all dismissal variants?
- [ ] "Speeding Fines Doubled" sign present (post-2008 zone)?
- [ ] Signs/markings/crosswalks match the current Standard Plans school series?
- [ ] Crossing-guard locations and ADA ramps coordinated, not assumed?
