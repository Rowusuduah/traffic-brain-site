description: What Vision Zero / High Injury Network vocabulary means in Tampa Bay scopes, and why HIN corridors drive safety screening.
---
**What:** Vision Zero = the policy goal of eliminating fatal and serious (K+A) injuries via the Safe System approach — crashes are treated as preventable outcomes of design, speed, and policy, not "accidents." The **High Injury Network (HIN)** is its screening tool: the small share of the street network carrying a disproportionate share of severe crashes, mapped and ranked to focus spending.

**Why:** Tampa, St. Petersburg, and Hillsborough County all run Vision Zero programs, so this vocabulary shows up verbatim in local scopes and RFPs. If your study corridor sits on the HIN, say so in the report — it's priority framing for the agency, aligns the rec with SS4A grant language, and the TPO already collects multimodal counts at HIN locations.

**When:** existing-conditions chapters ("corridor is on the adopted HIN"), safety study prioritization, calming petitions on HIN streets, grant support tasks.

**Local anchor:** [Plan Hillsborough Vision Zero](https://planhillsborough.org/vision-zero/) — the Hillsborough TPO's Action Plan (four tracks incl. "Paint Saves Lives"), Speed Management Plan, progress reports, and corridor studies for eight of the top-50 high-injury corridors `#status/verify` current plan edition and corridor list.
